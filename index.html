Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about student accommodation and safety on CampusNest.

How does CampusNest protect students from hostel scams?

Every accommodation listing is physically inspected or verified with student ambassadors on each campus. We confirm running water, electrical stability, and genuine ownership before granting the "Verified" badge.

Is browsing and contacting hostels free for students?

Yes! Browsing listings, saving favorites, and searching across Nigerian campuses is 100% free. You can directly connect with verified hostel managers via WhatsApp or phone call.

How does the Roommate Matching Hub work?

Students looking to split rent can post their budget and university department. You can review their study habits, clean preferences, and contact them directly to team up before renting.

How do I list my hostel or student service?

Click "List Property / Biz" in the menu and submit your details. Landlords and verified agents can activate placement with our automated Paystack system for instant visibility.

Need Help or Have Inquiries?

Our campus student support team is active 24/7 to help you resolve housing, complaints, or listing questions.
