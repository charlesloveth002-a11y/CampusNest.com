Frequently asked questions

Is CampusNest free for students?

Joining and browsing rooms is free. Premium is a small fee for 30 days (the price is shown before you pay) and unlocks saving rooms, contact details and listed businesses.

How do I contact a hostel or landlord?

Each listing shows contact details. Use them to ask questions or book a visit.

How do I save a room?

Tap the heart on a room card. Use “Show saved” to see only your saved rooms.

How can my business be listed?

Email us using the “List your business” section. Your business is listed after the listing fee is paid.
