Terms of Use

  1. What CampusNest is. CampusNest helps students find rooms, hostels and apartments, and shop from student businesses. We bring people together. We do not own, manage or guarantee any listing.
  2. Check before you pay. Visit a place and meet the agent or seller before you pay them. Never send money to someone you have not verified. CampusNest is not responsible for deals made between users.
  3. Accounts. Give true information and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  4. Agents and sellers. Post only true, current information and your own photos. Listings are reviewed and may be hidden or removed, including if they are reported as fake or misleading.
  5. Fees. Agents get a free first month. After that, posting and student premium access cost the fee shown in the app before you pay, and each payment gives 30 days of access. Contact us about any payment problem.
  6. Premium for students. Free accounts can browse. Premium unlocks saving rooms, contact details and the business list.
  7. Reports. If you see a fake or wrong listing, use "Report this listing". We review reports and may remove listings.
  8. No misuse. Do not post illegal, offensive or fake content, or try to break or abuse the site.
  9. Changes. We may update these terms and the site. Continued use means you accept the changes.
  10. Contact. charlesloveth002@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

  1. What we collect. Your name, school, email, account type (student or agent), the rooms you save, listings you post, and payment records. For bank-transfer payments we also store the receipt you upload.
  2. Why. To run your account, show listings, approve postings, confirm payments and keep the site safe.
  3. Where it is kept. Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Receipts are kept in a private area that only you and the CampusNest admin can open.
  4. Payments. Online card, transfer and USSD payments are handled by Paystack. CampusNest never sees or stores your card details.
  5. Who can see what. Listings you post are public once approved. Your contact details on a listing are shown to premium users. The CampusNest admin can see accounts and payment records. We do not sell your data.
  6. Cookies and storage. The site stores a login token in your browser so you stay logged in until you log out.
  7. Your choices. Email us to see, correct or delete your account and data.
